Visiting Thailand and wondering about dropshipping? You sell the thing; your supplier ships it straight to the buyer. People do well at it from Chiang Mai once they skip the rookie mistakes. eBay, a factory across the ocean, or Thai goods out through an import-export licence (Mot Dang has one). The money, the wait, the borders, and a snakes-and-ladders of rookie mistakes: https://motdang.net/sites/dropshipping/
