═══ SOURCES ═══

CDC, Head Lice — FAQ and Treatment pages (cdc.gov/lice/head-lice):
  crawl, can't hop or fly · head-to-head contact · hygiene plays no part ·
  pets don't spread them · nits within about 6 mm of the scalp · unlikely to
  spread in pools · follow the label, rinse over a sink, second treatment
  around day 9, comb every 2–3 days · check household members, treat those
  with lice at the same time · wash pillowcases, bag soft toys 2 weeks.
American Academy of Pediatrics, Head Lice clinical report (2022):
  a child with lice need not miss school; "no-nit" policies are discouraged.
