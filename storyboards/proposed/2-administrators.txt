Facts an administrator may want, with sources:
  · A nit is an egg glued to the hair. Viable nits sit within about 6 mm of the
    scalp; nits farther out are usually hatched or dead shells. (CDC)
  · Head lice are unlikely to spread through pool water. (CDC)
  · Lice spread by head-to-head contact. They don't hop or fly. (CDC)
  · The American Academy of Pediatrics (Head Lice clinical report, 2022): a
    child with lice need not be kept out of school, and "no-nit" policies are
    discouraged.
